# Japanorama

An unofficial summary of the Jet Lag season format.

## Objective

Two teams race from **Nishi-Ōyama Station** in Kagoshima to **Wakkanai** in Hokkaido. Winning requires both reaching Wakkanai and unlocking Hokkaido.

## Prefectures and challenges

1. Complete a challenge from the shared challenge bank in your current prefecture to unlock onward travel. Cards can also provide prefecture unlocks.
2. Follow the challenge's complete instructions, including attempt limits and automatic veto conditions.
3. A successful challenge lets you draw its stated number of power-up cards and **keep one**. Harder challenges offer more cards to choose from.

Power-up cards can accelerate travel or hinder opponents. Shinkansen access and prefecture unlocks can depend on the specific cards held; follow each card's wording.

## Using this tracker

The number shown as points is the challenge's card-draw reward, not a victory score. Manage the shared challenge bank, card draws, your hand, curses, permitted travel, and prefecture unlocks separately. The winner is determined by the race objective.

This page reveals and records one challenge at a time. Select **Complete** or **Failed** to resolve it. Start manual timers at the trigger in the challenge text. Retry waits, minimum durations, and multi-stage timing need separate tracking. Supply missing supporting materials listed in the note below the game.
