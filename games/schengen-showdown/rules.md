# Schengen Showdown

An unofficial summary of the Jet Lag season format. Agree on any local adaptations before playing.

## Objective and travel

- Two teams start in London and compete to own the most eligible European countries over six days.
- The board covers the Schengen Area and the included microstates: Andorra, Monaco, San Marino, and Vatican City.
- Use public transportation. Each team has a **US$4,000 flight budget**; trains, buses, and ferries do not spend that flight budget.

## Claiming and locking countries

1. Be the first team to enter a country to claim it provisionally. Flying over a country does not count.
2. Reveal its secret, country-specific challenge after arrival. Complete it to **lock** the country permanently.
3. The opposing team can steal an unlocked country by completing its challenge there. A locked country cannot be stolen.

The team owning the most countries at the end wins.

## Using this tracker

Choose the country card when ready to reveal its challenge. Resolve it with **Complete** or **Failed** before opening another card. Start manual timers at the trigger specified in the instructions.

The displayed 100-point rewards are illustrative. Track country ownership, locks, travel spending, the opponent's progress, and the session deadline separately. Some supporting challenge materials must be supplied separately; see the note below the game.
