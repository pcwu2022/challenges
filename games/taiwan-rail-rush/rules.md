# Taiwan: Rail Rush

An unofficial summary of the Jet Lag season format.

## Original game

- Two teams compete for **243 railway stations** over five days, playing 7:30 a.m.–5:30 p.m. Each starts in Taipei with 50 chips.
- Claim stations by travelling through them and depositing chips. To take an opponent's station, exceed its chip total. An increase is limited to five chips at a time; owned stations can be reinforced.
- Challenges award chips, steals, or multipliers. For **Call Your Shot**, commit to a target before attempting it.
- Challenge details remain secret until a team reaches the location and commits to attempting it.
- A failed challenge cannot be retried by that team. The other team may attempt it for a 50% larger reward.
- The map begins with three challenges. Completing one, or both teams failing it, removes it and generates two more, capped at ten active challenges.
- A team in debt can travel through enemy territory, but cannot claim those stations and must pay the opposing team.
- The team owning the most stations at 5:30 p.m. on the final day wins.

## Using this tracker

Reveal and resolve one challenge at a time. The interface displays fixed chip rewards as points. Track station ownership, reinforcements, chip spending, steals, multipliers, variable payouts, opponent rewards, and challenge availability separately.

Start manual timers at the trigger in the challenge text. Follow each challenge's own restrictions and provide any missing supporting materials listed in the note below the game.
